隐私政策 中文

生效日期：2025年1月1日　最近更新：2026年11月5日

感谢您使用 CG动画制作工具箱（以下简称"本应用"）。我们非常重视您的隐私，并致力于保护您的个人信息。本隐私政策描述了我们如何收集、使用和保护您的数据。本政策中关于错误日志的内容，同样适用于与本应用配合使用的嗜睡的猫咪系列软件（SSDMM，以及 Maya、Blender、Substance 3D Painter、Unreal Engine 插件）。

1. 我们收集的信息

本应用可能会收集以下类型的信息：

  • 个人信息（如适用）：当您自愿提供时，例如电子邮件地址、用户名等。您注册或登录会员时提供的账户信息，仅用于登录与授权验证。
  • 设备和使用数据：包括设备类型、操作系统、IP 地址、使用时间等，以便改进应用体验。
  • 日志数据：在应用崩溃或发生错误时，我们可能会收集相关日志信息，以便分析和修复问题。具体内容、上传条件与保存期限见第 2 条。

2. 错误日志的收集与上传

为了尽快发现并修复问题，本应用以及嗜睡的猫咪系列软件会在您的电脑上记录运行与错误日志。

  • 何时上传：仅在您已登录会员、且"出错时自动上传日志"处于开启状态（默认开启）时，在软件或插件出错、崩溃后，由本应用或 SSDMM 把相关错误报告上传到我们的服务器。未登录时不上传；登录、刷新登录状态、修改密码等过程中产生的日志只保存在您的电脑上，永不上传。各软件插件本身不联网。
  • 上传的内容：出错的软件或插件名称与版本；宿主软件（如 Maya、Blender、Unreal Engine、Substance 3D Painter）的版本；Windows 版本；出错时间与次数；错误信息与调用堆栈；以及您这次登录后、本次运行期间的日志（每份报告最多约 512 KB），其中可能包含出错时涉及的文件或工程路径。您的会员登录名用于把报告对应到您的账户。
  • 不会上传的内容：密码、令牌、授权码等凭据（写入日志前即自动遮蔽）；电脑的硬件标识；您的资产、工程、贴图、笔记等文件的内容；您登录之前或其他账户使用期间的日志。路径中的用户文件夹会以 %USERPROFILE% 代替。
  • 用途与保存期限：错误日志仅用于排查和修复问题，不用于广告或其他用途，也不会提供给第三方。服务器上的日志保存 72 小时后自动删除。
  • 如何关闭：您可以随时在本应用的"设置"中关闭"出错时自动上传日志"。该设置由嗜睡的猫咪系列软件共用；关闭后，日志只保存在您的电脑上（%LOCALAPPDATA%\ssdmm\logs），并会定期自动清理。

3. 我们如何使用信息

我们收集的信息可能用于以下用途：

  • 提供、维护和改进本应用的功能和用户体验。
  • 处理您的请求、反馈或技术支持需求。
  • 排查和修复软件与插件的错误和崩溃。
  • 分析使用趋势，以优化产品性能。
  • 遵守法律法规的要求。

4. 只在您电脑上处理的数据

  • 您的资产、模板、工程、笔记等内容只保存在您自己选择的资产库文件夹中，本应用不会上传。
  • 本应用与各软件插件之间的联动（例如"存入工具箱"、打开或导入资产库中的文件、显示各软件的连接状态）只通过您电脑上的本机文件夹 %LOCALAPPDATA%\ssdmm 进行，不经过网络。
  • 本应用的本地 API 只在本机（127.0.0.1）监听，只接受来自您这台电脑的请求。
  • 旧版云端笔记：旧版软件保存在我们服务器上的云端笔记，会在您登录并设置笔记库后转为本地笔记；转换并核对完成后，服务器上的副本会立即删除，不再保存，请妥善保管并备份您的笔记库。

5. 信息共享与披露

我们不会向第三方出售、租赁或共享您的个人信息，除非：

  • 经您明确同意。
  • 依照法律法规要求或执法机构的要求。
  • 为了保护我们的合法权益，如防止欺诈或安全威胁。

6. 数据存储与安全

我们采取合理的技术和管理措施保护您的信息安全，防止未经授权的访问、篡改或泄露。错误日志在服务器上只保存 72 小时。但请注意，任何数据传输和存储都无法保证 100% 安全。

7. 您的权利

您可以随时：

  • 请求访问、更正或删除您的个人信息。
  • 在本应用的"设置"中关闭"出错时自动上传日志"，或选择退出其他数据收集和使用（如适用）。
  • 通过下方联系方式联系我们获取更多信息。

8. 第三方服务

本应用可能包含指向第三方网站或服务的链接，这些网站或服务受其各自的隐私政策约束，我们不对此承担责任。

9. 隐私政策的变更

我们可能会不时更新本隐私政策，以反映我们的服务变化。更新后的隐私政策将通过应用或官方网站通知用户。

Privacy Policy English

Effective Date: January 1, 2025　Last Updated: November 5, 2026

Thank you for using CG Animation Toolbox (hereinafter referred to as "the Application"). We take your privacy very seriously and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your data. The parts of this policy about error logs also apply to the Sleepy Cat (嗜睡的猫咪) software used together with the Application (SSDMM and the plug-ins for Maya, Blender, Substance 3D Painter and Unreal Engine).

1. Information We Collect

The Application may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information (if applicable): When you voluntarily provide it, such as email address, username, etc. The account information you provide when you register or sign in as a member is used only for sign-in and authorization.
  • Device and Usage Data: Including device type, operating system, IP address, usage time, etc., to improve the application experience.
  • Log Data: When the Application crashes or encounters errors, we may collect relevant log information to analyze and fix issues. Section 2 describes what is included, when it is uploaded and how long it is kept.

2. Error Logs

To find and fix problems quickly, the Application and the other Sleepy Cat software record activity and error logs on your computer.

  • When logs are uploaded: Only when you are signed in as a member and "Upload error logs automatically" is turned on (it is on by default). After an error or crash in the software or a plug-in, the Application or SSDMM uploads the error report to our server. Nothing is uploaded when you are not signed in. Logs created while signing in, refreshing your sign-in or changing your password stay on your computer and are never uploaded. The plug-ins themselves never connect to the internet.
  • What is uploaded: the name and version of the software or plug-in; the version of the host application (such as Maya, Blender, Unreal Engine or Substance 3D Painter); the Windows version; when the error happened and how often; the error message and call stack; and the log of the current session since you signed in (up to about 512 KB per report), which may contain the paths of files or projects involved in the error. Your member sign-in name is used to link the report to your account.
  • What is never uploaded: passwords, tokens, authorization codes and other credentials (they are masked before anything is written to a log); hardware identifiers of your computer; the contents of your assets, projects, textures, notes or other files; logs from before you signed in or from another account. Your user folder in paths is replaced with %USERPROFILE%.
  • Purpose and retention: Error logs are used only to find and fix problems. They are not used for advertising or any other purpose and are not shared with third parties. Logs on our server are deleted automatically after 72 hours.
  • How to turn it off: You can turn off "Upload error logs automatically" in the Application's Settings at any time. The setting is shared by all Sleepy Cat software; when it is off, logs stay only on your computer (%LOCALAPPDATA%\ssdmm\logs) and are cleaned up automatically.

3. How We Use Information

The information we collect may be used for the following purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the features and user experience of the Application.
  • To process your requests, feedback, or technical support needs.
  • To find and fix errors and crashes in the software and plug-ins.
  • To analyze usage trends and optimize product performance.
  • To comply with legal and regulatory requirements.

4. Data That Stays on Your Computer

  • Your assets, templates, projects, notes and other content are stored only in the asset library folder you choose. The Application does not upload them.
  • The integration between the Application and the plug-ins (such as "Save to Toolbox", opening or importing files from the libraries, and showing whether each application is connected) runs only through the local folder %LOCALAPPDATA%\ssdmm on your computer, not over the network.
  • The Application's local API listens only on this computer (127.0.0.1) and accepts requests only from it.
  • Legacy cloud notes: Cloud notes that older versions stored on our server are converted to local notes after you sign in and set up a notes library. Once they have been converted and verified, the copy on our server is deleted immediately and is no longer kept, so please keep and back up your notes library.

5. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We will not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties unless:

  • With your explicit consent.
  • As required by law or law enforcement agencies.
  • To protect our legitimate rights, such as preventing fraud or security threats.

6. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and administrative measures to protect the security of your information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. Error logs are kept on our server for only 72 hours. However, please note that no data transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

7. Your Rights

You may at any time:

  • Request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information.
  • Turn off "Upload error logs automatically" in the Application's Settings, or opt out of other data collection and usage (if applicable).
  • Contact us using the information below for more information.

8. Third-Party Services

The Application may contain links to third-party websites or services, which are governed by their respective privacy policies. We are not responsible for them.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services. The updated Privacy Policy will be notified to users through the Application or our official website.
